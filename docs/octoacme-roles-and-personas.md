# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Managers / Scrum Masters

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers / Scrum Masters enable predictable team execution by facilitating delivery ceremonies, removing blockers, and improving team flow across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Track sprint health, delivery risks, and team capacity
- Escalate blockers and dependency risks to Project Managers when timeline or scope is impacted
- Coordinate with Product Managers on backlog readiness and with Developers and QA/Testing on execution clarity
- Keep Stakeholders informed on delivery confidence and near-term risks

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability and team throughput
- Reduce blocked work and unresolved dependencies
- Strengthen accountability for sprint commitments

### Typical Communication
- Daily coordination with Developers and QA/Testing on blockers
- Weekly syncs with Project Managers and Product Managers on progress and trade-offs
- Risk and escalation updates to Stakeholders when commitments are at risk

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear, testable requirements and help ensure shared understanding between business and delivery teams.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document requirements, workflows, and constraints
- Refine acceptance criteria with Product Managers and QA/Testing
- Clarify requirements for Developers during implementation
- Escalate requirement ambiguity, conflicting priorities, or scope gaps to Product Managers and Project Managers
- Coordinate with Stakeholders to validate business intent and expected outcomes

### Goals
- Improve requirement quality and reduce rework
- Increase alignment between business goals and delivered functionality
- Improve handoff quality across product, engineering, and testing

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement sessions with Product Managers and Developers
- Test planning alignment with QA/Testing
- Requirement validation checkpoints with Stakeholders and Project Managers

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects own solution direction and technical decision quality, ensuring delivery choices are scalable, maintainable, and aligned to project constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach, architecture boundaries, and key trade-offs
- Support Developers with implementation guidance and design reviews
- Partner with QA/Testing on quality strategy, testability, and non-functional requirements
- Coordinate with Product Managers and Project Managers on feasibility, dependencies, and sequencing
- Escalate high-impact technical risks and drive decision records for Stakeholder visibility

### Goals
- Reduce technical risk and unplanned rework
- Improve system quality, scalability, and maintainability
- Increase confidence in delivery estimates and implementation plans

### Typical Communication
- Technical planning and design review sessions with Developers and QA/Testing
- Feasibility and sequencing discussions with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Decision and risk communication to Stakeholders for major architecture impacts

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads ensure solutions are usable, accessible, and consistent with product experience standards throughout discovery and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define user flows, interaction patterns, and design specifications
- Collaborate with Product Managers on problem framing and success criteria
- Align with Developers on implementation details and design feasibility
- Partner with QA/Testing on usability, accessibility, and acceptance validation
- Escalate experience risks, scope trade-offs, or unresolved usability issues to Project Managers, Product Managers, and Stakeholders

### Goals
- Improve user satisfaction and task success rates
- Reduce usability defects and post-release design rework
- Ensure accessible and consistent product experiences

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Developers and QA/Testing
- Prioritization and trade-off discussions with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Experience rationale and impact updates for Stakeholders

---

## Support / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Support / Operations Leads prepare teams for production readiness and post-release reliability by coordinating operational requirements, incident response readiness, and service continuity.

### Responsibilities
- Define release readiness criteria, runbooks, and support handoff expectations
- Coordinate with Developers on observability, deployment safeguards, and rollback plans
- Partner with QA/Testing on release validation and operational test scenarios
- Align with Project Managers and Product Managers on release timing, support capacity, and risk windows
- Escalate production risks, incident trends, and customer-impacting issues to Stakeholders with recommended actions

### Goals
- Improve release stability and incident response effectiveness
- Reduce production disruptions and recovery time
- Strengthen accountability for operational ownership after launch

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness and go/no-go checkpoints with Project Managers, Developers, and QA/Testing
- Capacity and launch planning with Product Managers
- Incident and service health reporting to Stakeholders
